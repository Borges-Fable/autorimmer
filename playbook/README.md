# playbook/ — seed lessons, NOT a finished spec 4.1

These nine files were written by the orchestrator during the session-9 design
conversation with Evan. They are **raw material for 4.1**, deliberately committed
ahead of the spec so their citations are not lost — every one carries a
file+member or a live-bench observation behind it, and re-deriving that costs
more than restructuring it.

**What is here:** one lesson per file, frontmatter (name, trigger, severity,
confidence, source) plus what / why / how-to-apply / retire-when. Severity uses
vanilla's own three-tier vocabulary (`OpportunityType`: GoodToKnow, Important,
Critical) rather than a fresh scale, per this issue's amendment.

**What is NOT here, and is 4.1's job:** the checklist, `postmortem.md`, the
templates, the session-start load order, and the promotion criteria between
tiers. A first-draft checklist was written and then **deleted deliberately** —
Evan's call, and the right one: handing a worker a draft framing guarantees it
edits that framing instead of choosing a better one. The lessons are facts; the
checklist is a shape, and the shape should be thought about from scratch.

**These files are a starting point, not a contract.** 4.1's author may
restructure, merge, split or rewrite any of them. The one thing to preserve is
the citation behind each claim.

**One is a correction, and it is the interesting one.**
`growing-zone-default-is-potato.md` replaces an earlier lesson that said an unset
growing zone grows *nothing*. It grows potatoes. The old lesson pointed the agent
at a symptom that does not occur — worth keeping as the standing example of how a
plausible lesson fails.

**One has an open calibration that needs Evan.**
`combat-role-passion-over-skill.md` carries a proposed formula that is the
orchestrator's, not his, and says so in the file. Do not present it as his rule.
